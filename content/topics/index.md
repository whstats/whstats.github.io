The collection follows one statistical pipeline:

> [!theorem] Control, uniformity, estimation, transformation
> Control fluctuations of a fixed random quantity, extend that control to a class of functions, use the resulting uniform approximation to locate an estimator, and finally transform its limiting law.

## Concentration

[[sub-gaussian-tail-bounds|Sub-Gaussian Tail Bounds]] develops the moment-generating-function method, the Chernoff optimization, and the scaling of averages of independent sub-Gaussian variables.

## Empirical-process tools

[[symmetrization-and-rademacher-complexity|Symmetrization & Rademacher Complexity]] explains how a ghost sample and random signs make uniform deviations measurable through the richness of a function class.

## Estimation

[[m-estimation-consistency|M-Estimation Consistency]] shows why uniform convergence and a separated population optimum force an approximate empirical optimizer toward the target.

## Asymptotics

[[the-delta-method|The Delta Method]] shows how differentiability transports a root-$n$ limiting law through a smooth map.
